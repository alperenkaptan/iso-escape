Request account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Iso Escape game account and associated data, email info@sproutquest.co with the subject Iso Escape account and data deletion. Requests are handled manually. Opening this page does not delete anything.

You can make this request without reinstalling or opening the game. If you still have access, the game's Contact support option can prepare your player reference, Firebase support ID and app version for you to review before sending. If you do not have those details, describe the account and we will help identify it. A player ID alone is not proof of ownership.

What happens next

  1. Tell us whether your request concerns a guest profile, a linked Google Play Games game account, or both.
  2. We will review your request and may ask for proportionate information to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, payment-card details or full purchase receipts.
  3. After verification, we process the deletion and send confirmation, explaining any data that must be retained and why. We aim to respond within 30 days and will explain any extension required under applicable law.

Data covered by your request

The request covers the Firebase Authentication game account, its Firestore hint balance and cooldown data, guest-transfer links, reward and purchase-validation records associated with it, and related personal support data under our control. We also address associated data held by service providers on our behalf where applicable. Tell us if you want only particular data deleted rather than the whole game account.

Google Play Games saved progress, achievements and leaderboard entries may also be managed through Google's game-data deletion controls. We will explain any provider-specific steps needed for your request. Deleting an Iso Escape account does not delete your Google account.

Data retention and purchases

We do not retain your playable profile or hint balance after completing full account deletion. Limited transaction, security, anti-fraud or request records may be retained only where necessary for a legitimate purpose or legal obligation. We will identify any retained categories, purpose and applicable retention period in our response; these records are not used to restore your deleted game profile.

Deletion does not issue a refund or remove transaction records held independently by Google Play. Google Play manages purchase history and refunds. A valid non-consumable Remove Ads purchase may remain restorable through the purchasing store account.

Your device and future play

Signing out resumes your previous guest level progress on that device; hints already transferred to a linked account are not returned. Signing out, uninstalling or clearing app storage does not itself delete server records.

When deletion is completed, clear the game's storage on your devices and follow the instructions we provide for cloud saves. Retained device or cloud backups can otherwise upload old progress again. Starting the game or signing in afterwards can create a new game account. Device and OS backups have their own controls.

For data from earlier beta versions, mention the version if known. For other privacy questions, see our privacy policy or email the same address.
