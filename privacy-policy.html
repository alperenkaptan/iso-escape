Privacy policy

Iso Escape is a railway puzzle game published under the SproutQuest brand by an independent developer. For privacy requests, contact info@sproutquest.co.

Progress, sign-in and hint wallets

Level progress and settings are stored on your device. Optional Google Play Games sign-in synchronizes level progress through Google Saved Games. The game uses the Play Games player identifier to separate account saves. Your previous guest level progress is kept locally when you sign in and is resumed when you sign out; linked-account progress is kept separate.

The game uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions for hint wallets. Guest players receive an anonymous Firebase identity; Play Games sign-in uses a linked game identity. We process account identifiers, hint balances, cooldown timestamps, transaction identifiers and guest-transfer records to deliver hints, prevent duplicate rewards and keep balances separate. Guest hints can be transferred to a linked account once per guest wallet; transferred hints are not restored on sign-out. Spending a hint requires an internet connection. Firebase App Check with Play Integrity helps validate requests.

Purchases and advertisements

Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing process purchases. Purchase tokens and product information are sent to our Firebase functions for Google Play validation and delivery of hint purchases. Purchase and reward identifiers are retained to prevent duplicate grants. Remove Ads belongs to the purchasing store account, which may differ from the Play Games account. We do not receive payment-card numbers through the game.

The game uses Unity LevelPlay and mediated advertising providers, including Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Their SDKs may process advertising and device identifiers, network information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Rewarded-hint completion is verified through signed LevelPlay server callbacks using a game-generated installation identifier and reward-attempt identifier. Choosing not to watch an ad does not necessarily prevent SDK initialization or all SDK data collection. The game does not request precise GPS location.

Diagnostics and support

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Unity and platform services may process gameplay events, device and session information, crash reports and diagnostic data, subject to the game's available privacy choices and platform settings. These services help us understand usage and diagnose failures. If you send a support email or bug report, we receive your email address, message, app version, player reference, Firebase support ID when available and any logs you include. Review these before sending. Sending a support message is optional.

Providers and your choices

We use Google/Firebase and Unity services to operate the game. See Google's privacy policy and Unity's player privacy policy. Processing may occur outside your country. Device and provider privacy settings may offer advertising controls.

Guest level play is available offline; cloud synchronization, hint spending, purchases and rewarded ads require connectivity. Google Play Games sign-in is optional.

Account deletion and retention

You can request deletion of your game account and associated data at Account and data deletion, including without access to the app. Requests are handled manually and ownership may need to be verified. Signing out or uninstalling alone does not delete Firebase or Google Play Games records.

We keep account and gameplay data to provide the service until it is deleted. Limited records may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or resolving a request. The deletion page explains the scope and process; we explain any retained data and the applicable retention period when responding to your request. Provider records held independently, such as Google Play purchase history, have separate controls.

Earlier beta versions

Earlier builds used a separate game API and Unity Authentication. Their accounts are not imported into this version. Contact support about data from those versions. Older Google Saved Games hint balances may be imported once when an account first initializes its Firebase wallet.
